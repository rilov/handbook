Learn advanced machine learning algorithms through clear, step-by-step explanations and practical Python examples. This module focuses on two powerful classification techniques: Support Vector Machines (SVM) and Naive Bayes.

**Recommended Learning Path:**

1. **[SVM and Naive Bayes — A Friendly Guide]({{ site.baseurl }}/topics/advanced-machine-learning-svm-and-naive-bayes)** — Covers margin classifiers, support vectors, soft margin, kernels, Bayes' theorem, types of Naive Bayes, and worked demonstrations for email and letter classification.

**What You'll Learn:**

- Why maximising the margin leads to better classifiers
- How support vectors define the SVM decision boundary
- The role of the `C` parameter and soft margin
- The kernel trick and when to use linear, polynomial, and RBF kernels
- How to classify emails and letters using non-linear SVMs
- Bayes' theorem and how it drives Naive Bayes classification
- The naive independence assumption and why it works in practice
- Multinomial, Bernoulli, and Gaussian Naive Bayes
- Numerical stability with log-space computation
- How to choose the right Naive Bayes model for your data
- The strengths and weaknesses of SVM and Naive Bayes
- When to use SVM vs Naive Bayes vs other classifiers
